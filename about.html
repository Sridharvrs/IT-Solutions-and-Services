THE Stackly STORY

Started with a simple promise.

In 2012, two friends began helping small businesses solve their network and infrastructure challenges in Salem. Every problem solved brought a new opportunity to do more.

Over time, we expanded into cloud services, cybersecurity, software engineering and data solutions. What began as a small repair shop grew into a technology partner for ambitious businesses.

Our approach remains the same: understand the challenge, build thoughtfully, communicate openly and take ownership of the result.
